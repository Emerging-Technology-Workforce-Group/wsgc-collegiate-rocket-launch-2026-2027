Things to consider:
Budget:
* Cost of engine 
* Cost of additional hardware and drilling tools
Structure:
* How the engine will physically connect
* Minimum 5:1[MR1] thrust weight ratio 
i) Weight of materials used
ii) Weight of electronics 
iii) Weight of the actual engine 

RDDT/UDDT:
Rocket delay drilling tool
Universal delay drilling tool 
Used to time chute deployment of engine by cutting off segments of the engine starter 

DMS/RMS:
Disposable Motor System (Single use)
Reusable Motor System (Multi use)
